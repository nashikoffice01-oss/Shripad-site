How to start (2 minutes)

1

Find the file. Civil_RA.html is in your Downloads folder. Move it to a folder you will remember, such as Documents.

2

Open it with Chrome or Edge. Double-click the file, or right-click, Open with, Chrome or Edge. It works fully offline.

3

Register your name when asked, then upload your BOQ Excel file to create your first project.

4
